Our Services

TAC specializes in delivering high-quality services for civil infrastructure projects in Australia and overseas. Our core expertise lies in:

  • Cost Planning Services – Design Growth/Cost Planning, Quantity Surveying, Tender Validation, and monitoring cost movement through design stages.
  • Project Management – Scope management, procurement, financial management, scheduling (Primavera P6), and risk management.
  • Contract & Subcontract Management – Preparing and administering commercial contracts and subcontractor packages.
  • Feasibility Studies & Estimation – Providing accurate cost modelling and feasibility analysis for civil infrastructure projects.
