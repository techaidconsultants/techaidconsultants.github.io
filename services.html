Our Services

Comprehensive project management and cost planning solutions for civil infrastructure

Tech Aid Consultants specializes in delivering high-quality services for civil infrastructure projects in Australia and overseas. Our core expertise includes:

Design Growth/Cost Planning Services

TAC provides comprehensive cost planning services from tender stage through all design development phases. Our approach includes:

  • Quantity Surveying: Detailed measurement and quantification from design packages at each development stage
  • Cost Planning: Tracking and monitoring cost movement through design stages using customized Excel-based systems
  • Tender Validation: Verification of tender quantities and baseline establishment
  • Design Development Tracking: Monitoring scope changes and cost impacts through DCD, SDD, FDD, and IFC stages

TAC has successfully delivered cost planning services on major projects including the Sydney Metro, WestConnex projects, and Brisbane Airport Link.
