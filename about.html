About Tech Aid Consultants

Cost, commercial and project controls support for major civil infrastructure

Who We Are

Tech Aid Consultants (TAC) is a civil infrastructure consultancy specialising in cost planning, cost reconciliation and project controls.

For over 20 years, TAC has supported contractors and joint ventures on major motorway, rail and tunnelling projects in New South Wales, Queensland and Victoria. Our work sits between the estimate, the design and the delivery team – tracking how cost moves from tender through design development and construction, and reporting it in a form project teams can act on.

Our Australian project experience is supported by a technical team in Pakistan and the UAE. This allows us to provide experienced, responsive support at a competitive cost, and to scale resources up during design delivery peaks.

What We Do

Our services cover the cost and commercial side of project delivery, from tender through to completion. See our services in detail.

Project Cost Reconciliation

Separating cost movement on alliance and collaborative contracts into design development, scope change and delivery conditions.

Cost Planning & QS

Design growth tracking, quantity take-off and tender validation through each design stage.

Project Controls

Primavera P6 scheduling, progress and earned value reporting, and project systems.

Commercial & Risk

Contract and subcontract administration, risk registers and value management.

How We Work

Establish the baseline

Rebuild the tender estimate in a structured, linked format with quantities, rates and assumptions.

Measure

Quantify each design package or delivery update against the baseline.

Track

Identify what has changed, by package and by cause, and why.

Report

Provide clear reporting in an agreed format for the project team's review and decisions.
