About Tech Aid Consultants

Over 20 years of delivering specialized civil consultancy services

Executive Summary

Tech Aid Consultants (TAC) is a specialized project management consultancy providing services to the Australian civil infrastructure industry for over 20 years. TAC is a civil consultancy with offices based in Pakistan and UAE, specializing in delivering comprehensive services relating to civil infrastructure projects to Australian and overseas clients.

TAC's strength lies in its flexibility and responsiveness in meeting client expectations. We possess a highly experienced team with a significant track record in cost planning services on similar projects for clients in Australia. TAC provides demonstrated ability to deliver technical expertise, industry skills, together with outstanding client service and relationship management focus.

Our Capabilities

TAC specializes in delivering the following services relating to civil infrastructure projects:

  • Project Management, including Scope Management, Procurement and Financial Management
  • Contract Management, including preparing and administering commercial contracts
  • Sub-contract management including preparing and managing sub-contractor packages
  • Feasibility Studies, Project Estimation, and Design Growth/Cost Planning
  • Project Planning, Scheduling and Control using Primavera P6
  • Project Systems Development, Implementation and Management
  • Risk and Opportunity Management including Value Management
