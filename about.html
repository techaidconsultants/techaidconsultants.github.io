About Tech Aid Consultants

Engineers, cost estimators and project managers serving Pakistan, the UAE and Australia

Who We Are

Tech Aid Consultants (TAC) is a multidisciplinary consultancy of professional engineers, cost estimators, planners and project managers.

We provide project management, commercial and cost services for infrastructure and construction projects, working with contractors, joint ventures and project owners in Pakistan, the United Arab Emirates and Australia.

Our team has more than 20 years of experience across the project lifecycle – from feasibility and tender, through design development and construction, to completion. In Australia, this includes major motorway, rail and tunnelling projects for leading contractors and joint ventures in New South Wales, Queensland and Victoria.
